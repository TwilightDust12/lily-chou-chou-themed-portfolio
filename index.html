Identity & Vision

About Philia & The Ether

A personal portfolio forged in the quiet intersection between early 2000s cyber-solitude, Debussy classical phrasing, and modern creative web technology.

Recreating the Atmosphere of Lily Chou-Chou

In Shunji Iwai’s landmark 2001 film All About Lily Chou-Chou, a high school boy escapes the brutal pressures of his reality by stepping into vast, sun-drenched rice fields with his Discman headphones. On the internet BBS board, under the handle Philia, he discovers the “Ether” (エーテル)—a cosmic, emotional frequency where pain dissolves into musical purity.

As a creator, I take this cinematographic philosophy as my design manifesto. In an era dominated by relentless algorithms and attention-seeking feeds, I design digital sanctuaries: web spaces that breathe, soundscapes that envelop, and interfaces that respect human stillness and contemplation.

“The Ether is infinite. When you surrender your thoughts to the vibration, even solitude feels sacred.”
— Philia, BBS Forum Entry #012

The Ether Knowledgebase & FAQ

This portfolio is an homage to Shunji Iwai’s masterpiece All About Lily Chou-Chou. The website captures its core visual motifs: glowing sunlight piercing through summer green rice fields, the tactile romance of carrying a Discman, and the quiet dignity of seeking emotional connection across online communities.

I prioritize pure semantic craftsmanship: zero bloated third-party dependencies, fluid responsiveness across all screens, full keyboard accessibility, and subtle atmospheric motion. Sound is treated as an equal partner to visual layout.

In the film’s mythology, the Ether is the metaphysical medium through which true emotion travels. It can be radiant and restorative (the blue ether) or crushing and suffocating (the red ether). This website was built to channel the restorative blue ether into modern web architecture.
